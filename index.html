Fundamental Analysis

Revenue

Revenue growth helps evaluate the company's business performance and growth potential.

Profit

Profitability shows the company's ability to generate earnings from its business operations.

P/E Ratio

Price-to-Earnings ratio helps investors understand the valuation of a company's stock.

Debt

Debt levels help in understanding the financial risk and stability of a company.

Technical Analysis

Technical analysis studies historical price and trading volume patterns. Common indicators include Moving Average, RSI and MACD.

Risk Factors

Market Risk

Stock prices can change because of overall market conditions.

Economic Risk

Inflation, interest rates and economic changes can affect stocks.

Company Risk

Business performance and management decisions can affect stock value.

Conclusion

Stock market analysis helps investors understand company performance, valuation, risk and market trends. Investors should conduct proper research before making investment decisions. Past performance does not guarantee future returns.

📚 About the Project

This Stock Market Analysis project is developed as an academic project to study the performance of major Indian companies and understand the basic concepts of stock market analysis.

🎯 Objectives

Objective 1

To understand the basic concepts of the Indian stock market.

Objective 2

To compare the performance of major Indian companies.

Objective 3

To understand fundamental and technical analysis.

Objective 4

To study the risks involved in stock market investment.

📝 Methodology

The project uses a descriptive approach to study selected Indian companies. Company sectors, fundamental factors, technical indicators, risk factors and sample trends are considered for academic analysis.


The analysis focuses on concepts such as revenue, profit, P/E ratio, debt, moving averages, RSI and MACD.
